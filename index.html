Model assumptions

  • Rigid arm; all selected parts move together about one fixed revolute axis.
  • DC motor steady-state electrical model with winding resistance and back EMF; current is clamped per motor.
  • Ideal reduction apart from user-specified efficiency; no gearbox backlash, compliance, friction, or thermal dynamics.
  • Time-optimal bang-bang-style feedback simulation; finite timestep and terminal tolerances apply.
  • Battery voltage is treated as fixed at the configured bus voltage (no battery internal-resistance sag model yet).
